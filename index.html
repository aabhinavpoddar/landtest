Before you begin

Good advice starts with the right questions.

A few things worth knowing before commissioning a review.

Does MPD-2047 automatically make every unplanned property eligible?

No. Chapter 4.6 identifies categories and exclusions. Eligibility, applicable regulations, title/rights, location-specific restrictions and statutory approvals must be checked for the particular site.

What should I share for an initial review?

If available, share the site address or location, approximate area, land-use/current-use information, ownership or title documents, colony/cluster status, access-road details, and any notices, plans or prior applications. Do not send original documents at this stage.

Can you guarantee regularisation, FAR or approval?

No. Outcomes depend on the applicable notified regulations, competent authorities, site conditions, required clearances and professional review. This service is advisory and does not promise any statutory outcome.

What does the first engagement deliver?

A scoped initial review can be used to produce a written issue list, a preliminary policy-pathway note, a document checklist and recommended next steps. The exact deliverable and fee should be agreed before work starts.
